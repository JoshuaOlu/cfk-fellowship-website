What is the CFK Fellowship

Not a programme. A community you grow into.

The Fellowship is a long-term commitment to growth, service, and meaningful contribution. Structured enough to develop you, and open enough to make room for who you actually are.

You do real work from day one
Fellows are active contributors within CFK's programmes from the moment they join. The work you do does not stay in a folder — it goes out into the world and is used by real students.
You grow alongside people who care
The Fellowship is a community. Light Sessions, team collaboration, and a shared mission create relationships that last well beyond your active time with CFK.
You advance at your own pace
Four Fellowship levels, each with clear expectations and real recognition. You move forward when you are ready, not on an arbitrary clock.
You are treated like a person
CFK's Fellowship Companion documents your rights as a Fellow alongside what we expect from you. The relationship is mutual, and we put that in writing.
Where You Will Contribute

Find the track that fits you.

Every Fellow works within one of four tracks. Read each one carefully and think about where your skills and interests belong.

WikiQuestions

Digitise and publish past SSCE examination questions on wikiquestions.org, making them freely searchable and accessible to students across Nigeria. A laptop is a firm requirement for this track.

Laptop Required

CTRI

Write free, openly available textbooks for Nigerian secondary school students preparing for WAEC, JAMB, and NECO. Currently focused on Mathematics, Physics, Biology, Chemistry, and Agric Science.

Textbook Writing

Model National Assembly

Help plan and run a high-fidelity legislative simulation set in the fictional Federal Republic of Mona. Designed to educate young Nigerians about governance, ethics, and civic responsibility.

Events and Programme

Admin and Support

Keep everything running. Roles span administration, finance, fundraising, social media, graphic design, video editing, and more. If your skills serve CFK's mission without fitting the other tracks, this is where you belong.

Multiple Roles
The Fellowship Journey

Where you start, and how far you can go.

Every Fellow begins in the same place. What comes next depends on how much you put in.

Start Here
16 wks

Foundation Cohort

The most intensive phase of your Fellowship. You are active within your track, attending workshops, Light Sessions, and weekly reflections. Fellows who complete this phase with genuine participation receive the CFK Foundation Cohort Certificate.

Level 1
6 mo

Foundation Fellow

Fellows who continue after the cohort remain at Foundation level for the rest of their minimum six months. Teams, bi-monthly Light Sessions, and your first Advancement Portfolio.

Level 2
12 mo

Associate Fellow

Consistent contribution, growing ownership, and emerging leadership. You take on more within your team and begin to support newer Fellows around you.

Level 3
18 mo

Senior Fellow

Strategic contribution, people development, and sustained impact. Senior Fellows are eligible for a Sabbatical to pursue a personal, impact-driven project outside CFK.

Level 4
No limit

Principal Fellow

The highest level. A record of sustained leadership and long-term commitment. Principal Fellows who eventually step away are honoured as Emeritus Fellows — a title they carry permanently.
